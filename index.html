Viola De Ruvo

Computational & theoretical neuroscience · Machine learning · Statistical physics

M.Sc. Artificial Intelligence, Bocconi University, Milan
Research Assistant, Brunel group, Department of Computing Sciences

I am a physicist turned computational neuroscientist, working where physics, machine learning and the brain meet. I am based at Bocconi University in Milan, where I am doing my M.Sc. in Artificial Intelligence and work as a research assistant in Prof. Nicolas Brunel's group. I studied physics at Sapienza University of Rome, where I gained strong foundations in mathematical and statistical physics that I now use to build and analyze models of neural circuits.

My research asks how much of what a neural circuit computes follows from random synaptic connectivity, and what needs structure and learning. I study this question in the cerebellum, fitting rate models of the circuit to recordings from behaving monkeys. I have also worked in natural language processing, building Detoxify-IT, the first Italian dataset for text detoxification.
